DeskSmash is a native macOS desktop toy inspired by Miroslav Němeček’s Desktop Games / Desktop Destroyer. Smash, scorch, and paint over your windows, or take a calmer break with bubbles and confetti. Clear it all in a click.

![DeskSmash toolbar with native window controls, tool selection, and Classic and Peaceful modes](docs/assets/toolbar-1.1.png)

## Turn your desktop into a playground

DeskSmash draws an interactive overlay over your desktop and open windows. Pick a tool, then click or drag to crack the screen, leave scorch marks, splash paint, or scatter confetti across whatever you have open.

The mess belongs to that overlay. Your actual files and windows stay untouched. Wipe away a patch with the Cleaning Cloth, reset the whole scene, or toggle Click-Through to get back to the windows underneath.

## Sixteen tools. Two ways to play.

Each mode has ten tools, with four shared between them. Switch whenever you want a different kind of break.

### Classic

Smash, saw, shoot, and scorch your desktop overlay. Critters react to weapon hits and flames.

**Tools:** Hammer, Chainsaw, Machine Gun, Flamethrower, Baseball Bat, Color Thrower, Phaser, Stamp, Critters, and Cleaning Cloth.

### Peaceful

Play with ribbons, confetti, bubbles, and little games. Critters stay unharmed in Peaceful mode.

**Tools:** Ribbon, Stamp, Biscuit, Confetti Gun, Critters, Juggle Ball, Color Thrower, Leafblower, Bubble Gun, and Cleaning Cloth.

## Made for Mac

- **Native controls:** Close or minimize the app, drag the toolbar by its empty space, and toggle Click-Through to use the windows underneath.
- **Sound and feel:** Positional audio follows the action, with optional haptic feedback on supported Mac trackpads.
- **Your own settings:** Pick colors and stamps, adjust the bubble mixture and cloth size, and set the volume.
- **Clean up your way:** Wipe away a small patch with the Cleaning Cloth, or use Reset to clear the entire scene, including critters and loose pieces.

The app does not require an account and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Read the [Privacy Policy](https://desksmash.ygtars.com/privacy) for details.

## Download and install

**[Download the latest DeskSmash DMG](https://github.com/ygtarslan/desksmash-releases/releases/latest/download/DeskSmash-latest.dmg)**

1. Download the DMG and open it.

## An old desktop memory, rebuilt for my Mac

As a kid, I played Desktop Games / Desktop Destroyer on the computer at my parents’ pharmacy. That little Windows toy by Miroslav Němeček of Gemtree Software let you take a hammer, a chainsaw, or a flamethrower to your desktop. It was simple, offline fun, and it stuck with me.

Years later, I wanted that feeling on my Mac. I couldn’t find an alternative that felt right, so I built DeskSmash. It is heavily inspired by the original, redesigned and reimagined as a modern, native macOS app.

The idea is still a short, playful break from whatever is on your screen.
